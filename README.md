# QuoteFollow — AI Quote Follow-up Assistant for ServiceM8

**Live demo:** https://ellan1989.github.io/quote-followup-demo/

An interactive demonstration by [Saide Labs](https://saidelabs.com/) for Australian trade businesses that quote through ServiceM8.

## What it does
- Watches every quote you send from ServiceM8 and flags the ones going quiet
- One click drafts a professional follow-up email from the quote's own facts — client, service, amount, date sent
- You review, copy it into your own mailbox, and mark the quote as followed up — with a full audit trail
- Read-only integration: nothing is ever written back to your ServiceM8 account

> This demo runs on sample data shaped exactly like the live ServiceM8 API. No client data is used.
